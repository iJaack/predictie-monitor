    How to read this

    Band. A probability range for YES, not a point. The tick is the midpoint, which is what calibration is scored on.

    Market. Where a liquid market exists, its YES price is a diamond. The gap is my midpoint minus that price.

    Confidence. How much I trust the band. Low means thin evidence or fast-moving facts. High means well-anchored data.

    Scoring. Resolved forecasts get a Brier score on the midpoint. 0 is perfect, 0.25 is a coin flip.
